Fit Track Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Fit Track is a private workout app made for one family. It shows each day's workout from the family's coach, logs sets on iPhone and Apple Watch, measures heart rate during workouts, and saves workouts to Apple Health. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or rents information to anyone.

What Fit Track records

  • The workouts you log: exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, times, distances and notes.
  • Heart rate and active calories during workouts, from Apple Watch or compatible earbuds through Apple Health.
  • Age, sex, height and weight from Apple Health, to estimate calories on your phone.
  • Body weight, body fat, waist size and your age from Apple Health, only if you turn on Share Health Data with Coach.
  • The gyms and equipment you enter, including any photos you take of equipment.
  • A record of which screens and buttons you used, without any of the values you entered. It stays on your phone and leaves it only if you choose Send Diagnostics.

Where it is kept

Your information is kept on your iPhone, in Apple Health when you allow it, and in your own private iCloud account. Apple Watch shows your workout while it runs and keeps nothing. Fit Track has no servers of its own.

Who can see it

No one, until you choose Connect to Coach, which asks first. Connecting shares your Fit Track information in your iCloud account with one computer: the family's coaching computer, which plans your workouts and keeps a backup copy of what you send. The link you send admits that computer alone.

The coaching computer uses Claude, an AI service from Anthropic, to read your workouts, including heart rate, and write your plans. What it reads is sent to Anthropic for that alone. Anthropic's handling of it is described in Anthropic's privacy policy.

If you turn on Share Health Data with Coach in Fit Track's Settings, the coaching computer also receives your body weight, body fat, waist size, your age in years (never your birth date) and the workouts other apps saved to Apple Health, for its charts and heart-rate zones. It is off until you turn it on, and turning it off stops it.

Your information is not shared with anyone else. If you use Fit Track on your own, without connecting, it stays on your phone, in Apple Health when you allow it, and in your own iCloud account.

Apple Health

With your permission, Fit Track reads heart rate, active energy, body measurements and workouts saved by other apps, to show your workouts and estimate calories, and it saves your finished workouts to Apple Health. Health information is never used for advertising or marketing, and is shared only as described above: with the family's coaching computer and its AI service, once you allow it.

Your choices

  • Stop sharing with the coaching computer and its AI service at any time: in Fit Track, Settings, then Support, then Disconnect from Coach.
  • Change what Fit Track may read or write in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps.
  • Delete Fit Track's information from your iCloud account in Settings: tap your name, then iCloud, then the storage list, where Fit Track appears.
  • Deleting the app removes the information kept on your phone.
  • To have your information removed from the coaching computer, or for any question about this policy, write to the address below.
